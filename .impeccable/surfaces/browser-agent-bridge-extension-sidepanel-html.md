# AXIS conversation workspace

Mode: Operate. Scope: packaged side panel, history, settings, and approval popup.
Audience: desktop browser users delegating work while retaining informed control.
Constraints: narrow panels, offline assets, existing backend and approval semantics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Put the conversation first and make the next available action clear. Keep every capability, revealing activity and advanced controls on demand.

OWN-WORLD: User-selected Oracle Redwood: warm neutral light surfaces, charcoal dark surfaces and header, restrained red actions, local sans typography, rectangular controls, and the existing AXIS mark.

STORY: Read the answer, understand current progress, attach evidence or follow up, and review consequential browser changes before acting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 52px header, compact conversation title, dominant answer timeline, then one current-task strip above the composer. Attachment and Send anchor the input; Approval/Automatic stays visible. Settings leads with Connection and Appearance.

FORM: Conversation-first operating workspace, pinned by the user's approved plan. Code-led; no seed or comp selection because direction and composition were explicitly selected.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
